Who We Are

Established in 1989 in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, RJ HEAT TECH SERVICES has grown into a leading manufacturer and service provider for all types of heat treatment furnaces and allied industrial equipment.

Our commitment to custom engineering, reliability, and technical innovation has made us a trusted partner to hundreds of clients across various industrial sectors including automotive, aerospace, foundry, and steel processing.

"With over 35 years of field experience, we don't just build furnaces—we engineer high-performance thermal solutions designed for maximum efficiency and longevity."
