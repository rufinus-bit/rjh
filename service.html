Our Service Capabilities

At RJ HEAT TECH SERVICES, we don't just manufacture new furnaces; we provide comprehensive repair, upgrade, and maintenance services for existing systems of any make or brand. Our engineering team can analyze performance issues and restore your equipment to optimal working condition.

Heating Element Replacement

Expert winding and replacement of high-quality Nichrome, Kanthal, or custom alloy heating elements to restore original thermal capacity.

Refractory & Insulation Relining

Complete or partial relining using high-temperature ceramic fiber modules, firebricks, and castables to improve energy efficiency.

Control Panel Upgrades

Modernizing legacy control panels with advanced PID temperature controllers, PLC systems, thyristor power controllers, and touchscreens.

Capacity & Temp Upgrades

Engineering modifications to increase chamber capacity, improve temperature uniformity, or elevate maximum operating temperatures.

Thermocouple & Sensor Calibration

Replacing, calibrating, and testing temperature sensors and instrumentation to comply with standard heat treatment certifications.

Mechanical Overhauls

Re-building doors, pneumatic/hydraulic lifting systems, conveyor belts, and trolley drives for bogie-type furnaces.
